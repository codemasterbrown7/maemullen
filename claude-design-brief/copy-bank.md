# Copy bank

Every piece of real copy that exists today, from the client's deck. Treat it as raw material:
draw on whatever serves the design, and **write your own supporting copy where the layout needs
more** — headlines, eyebrows, section intros, microcopy, work descriptions. Final copy gets
client sign-off later, so favour a good-looking, natural layout over strict fidelity.

Voice and casing are the design system's, not this file's. Two things it doesn't cover:
**British English** (*organised*, *personalise*, £ sterling), and **no prices** — whether pricing
appears on the site at all is unresolved with the client.

The deck writes the studio name as "Maemullen"; the wordmark reads "MaeMüllen". Which form wins
in prose is unresolved, so either is acceptable in copy you write.
